# NIAR Web Scene Patterns

Use these as starting points, not rigid templates.

## Cover

- Background: `cover-gradient-wave-logo.png`.
- Navy panel on left.
- Large white title.
- Gold subtitle.
- Short orange rule under title.
- Reveals: title -> subtitle -> date/source or key phrase.

## Dark Key Message

- Background: `dark-section-bokeh-logo.png`.
- White title and short kicker.
- Three navy cards or one large quote panel.
- Reveals: title -> key sentence -> cards one by one.

## White Two-Column

- Background: `white-content-wave-logo.png` or `light-title-cream-logo.png`.
- Navy title, orange rule.
- Two white or lightly bordered panels.
- Reveals: left panel -> right panel -> summary bar.

## Strategy Cards

- Background: `white-content-wave-logo.png`.
- 2x2 cards with colored number bars.
- Reveals: each card appears in sequence, then a bottom conclusion.

## Photo + Evidence

- Background: `white-content-wave-logo.png`.
- Left stacked callouts, right framed photo.
- Caption below photo.
- Reveals: photo -> callouts one by one -> conclusion.

## Timeline / Roadmap

- Background: `white-footer-line-logo.png`.
- Horizontal line across center.
- Milestones alternate above/below.
- Reveals: line -> milestones one by one -> final sentence.

## Table / Data

- Background: `white-content-wave-logo.png`.
- Use editable HTML/CSS table.
- Header row in orange or navy.
- Reveals: title -> table rows grouped by meaning -> key takeaway.

## Network / Ecosystem

- Background: `closing-gradient-wave-logo.png`.
- Center navy node and surrounding partner cards.
- Thin gold/cyan connector lines.
- Reveals: center -> partners -> connectors -> conclusion.

## Closing

- Background: `closing-gradient-wave-logo.png`.
- Left navy panel with large white closing statement.
- Optional framed photo or final evidence card on right.
- Reveals: closing title -> final sentence -> source/contact if needed.
