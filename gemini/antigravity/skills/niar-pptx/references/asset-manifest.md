# NIAR Background Asset Manifest

All background assets live in `assets/backgrounds/` and should usually be inserted at `x=0`, `y=0`, `w=13.333 in`, `h=7.5 in` unless noted. Most assets already include the NIAR logo in the top-right corner.

## Logo Area

The logo is baked into the full-slide backgrounds. Treat the top-right `x=10.4-13.0 in`, `y=0.25-0.95 in` as protected. Do not place text, photos, or shapes there.

## Footer Area

Reserve `y=6.85-7.15 in` for page number and footer. On white slides, use navy/gray footer text. On dark or gradient slides, use white or cream footer text.
